A Senior Director & Distinguished Engineer at Google, Antonio Gulli, has just released a 400+ page free book:  
 📖 Agentic Design Patterns: A Hands-On Guide to Building Intelligent Systems  
This isn’t just another AI theory book — it’s a practical blueprint for building agentic AI systems that go beyond simple prompting.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*12s3zdt0j9NO2YbtFkeDwQ.jpeg)

Cover Photo

### Why this matters:

🔹 Covers 21 powerful patterns — from Prompt Chaining and Tool Use to Planning, Multi-Agent collaboration, Safety & Guardrails, Reflection, and more  
 🔹 Includes hands-on examples, diagrams, and reusable code with frameworks like LangChain, CrewAI, and Google’s Agent Developer Kit  
 🔹 Moves from LLM capabilities → structured, production-ready AI agents  
 🔹 Profits from the official edition (coming Dec 2025) will support Save the Children 🌍

### Book structure at a glance:

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*1VBnLh6YNqR7JZLAm54SJQ.png)

Table of Contents

📌 Part I: Core patterns (Prompt Chaining, Routing, Reflection, Planning)  
 📌 Part II: Memory, Adaptation, Model Context Protocol (MCP), Goal setting  
 📌 Part III: Human-in-the-loop, RAG, Exception handling  
 📌 Part IV: Advanced topics — A2A communication, Safety, Evaluation, Optimization  
 📌 Appendices: Frameworks, CLI/GUI agents, Glossary
