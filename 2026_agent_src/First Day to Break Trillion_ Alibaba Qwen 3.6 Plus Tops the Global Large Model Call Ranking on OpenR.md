# First Day to Break Trillion! Alibaba Qwen 3.6 Plus Tops the Global Large Model Call Ranking on OpenRouter

![aibase](/_nuxt/userlogo.q1jFctRw.png)

Published in Latest AI News

Time :Apr 7, 2026

Read :4minute

The Large Model API Calling Platform OpenRouter has released the latest weekly ranking. **Alibaba Qwen3.6-Plus** successfully ranked first in global large model weekly usage thanks to its high developer activity, and has been on the daily ranking list for four consecutive days.

### Key Breakthrough: Daily Usage Enters the "Trillion" Era

This top position is not only a ranking milestone but also a qualitative change in usage volume:

**Milestone Record:**Qwen3.6-Plus has become the first model on the OpenRouter platform to exceed **10 trillion Tokens** in daily usage.

**Dominant Performance:** In the competitive global model library, the Qwen series has demonstrated strong ecosystem stickiness, becoming one of the preferred underlying bases for global developers.

### Industry Background: The Explosive Growth of Domestic Large Models Abroad

On this international mainstream API distribution platform, OpenRouter, Chinese large models represented by Alibaba Qwen are rapidly reshaping the market landscape through high cost-effectiveness and fast technological iteration. A daily consumption of 10 trillion Tokens indicates that they have already deeply penetrated high-frequency application scenarios such as autonomous driving, intelligent agents, and large-scale content production.

### Capital and Ecosystem: Re-evaluation of Value Amid the Computing Power Trend

At the same time as Alibaba's large model topped the rankings, the capital market also gave a warm response:

**Market Enthusiasm:** On April 7th, the total trading volume of the Shenzhen and Shanghai stock markets broke through 1 trillion yuan, showing the high attention of funds on the technology and AI computing power sectors.

**Market Segmentation:** In contrast, Anthropic's **Mythos** model was recently reported to face development challenges due to excessive computing power consumption, while **Hongxiong AI**, which focuses on physical AI, successfully secured 210 million yuan in funding.

**Conclusion: The "Chinese Moment" of AI Applications**

From following technology to leading in usage volume, domestic forces represented by Alibaba Qwen are redefining the new pace of global AI commercialization. With the continuous decline in Token costs and the exponential growth in usage scale, a global AI application ecosystem driven by Chinese models is accelerating its formation.
