## Abstract

  

This IDC Market Perspective details that the observability and AIOps market is undergoing a significant transformation as digital business operations become central to enterprise value creation. In 2026, observability and AIOps vendors will face rapid shifts driven by digital business expansion, overwhelming data volumes, and the rise of agentic AI architectures. Key trends include intensified integration, AI-optimized system design, resource shortages, the growing importance of context in telemetry, and cost management as a core platform feature. Vendors must adapt by partnering with customers, enabling organizational change, and embedding governance and efficiency into their solutions to remain competitive in an increasingly complex digital landscape.

"As digital complexity outpaces human control, will observability and AI-driven automation redefine not just IT operations but the very core of business resilience?," said Shannon Kalvar, research director for Enterprise System Management, Observability, and AIOps, IDC.
