# Huawei will launch the first AI-Native framework for intelligent operations and a new generation of solutions at MWC 2026

[Barcelona, Spain, March 1, 2026] Advancing all intelligence, Huawei will officially release the industry's first AI-Native framework for the intelligent operations and a new generation of intelligent solutions during MWC 2026.

With the rapid advancement of emerging technologies such as Large Language Models (LLMs), World Models, and Digital Twins, the solutions and value propositions in the operations domain are being constantly reshaped. Following a year-long collaboration with industry organizations and several leading global operators, Huawei has completed the industry's first AI-Native framework definition for the intelligent operations field.

![260301-07](https://www-file.huawei.com/admin/asset/v1/pro/view/312b3ed6a3a94389bb8b8968f83e882e.jpg)

Huawei believes that AI-Native is a value-driven systematic framework, which consists of three core elements:

* **Element 1: Outcome Oriented**. Addressing challenges that were unsolvable through traditional methods, or tackling new problems in emerging scenarios to create incremental value.
* **Element 2: DTN & Domain Model Driven**. Building digital twins and domain- models for the telecom industry to drive solutions achieve a closed-loop of value outcomes.
* **Element 3: Agentic Operations**. Optimizing existing operational workflows, organizational structures, talent composition, and business models to enable efficient synergy between human experts and digital employees, thereby unleashing AI-driven "New Quality Productive Forces."

Based on the AI-Native framework, Huawei is set to release a new generation of intelligent operations solutions. These solutions will establish new paradigms, release new value, and achieve a leap in all intelligence.

On March 2, 2026, Huawei cordially invites industry organizations and media partners to gather in Barcelona to witness the grand launch of the new generation of solutions.
