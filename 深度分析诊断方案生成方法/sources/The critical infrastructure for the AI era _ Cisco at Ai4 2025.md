### Day 3 keynote: The catalyst for driving an agentic AI revolution | Fireside chat

Jeetu Patel, President and Chief Product Officer, Cisco  
Matt Egan, Senior Business and Economy Reporter, CNN  
Wednesday, August 13 | 8:54-9:12 a.m. PT | MGM Grand Garden Arena

Join Jeetu Patel, President and Chief Product Officer at Cisco, for a fireside chat with Matt Egan, award-winning CNN Senior Reporter, where they will explore the transformative power of agentic AI. They will discuss how organizations that effectively harness agentic AI will set themselves apart in the near future, while those that don't risk falling behind. Together, Jeetu and Matt will discuss the evolving landscape of safety, security, identity, and human collaboration with autonomous AI agents. Jeetu will also share Cisco's groundbreaking innovations that secure agentic AI ecosystems at machine scale, turning security from a challenge into a strategic advantage that accelerates AI adoption. Discover how these advancements empower organizations to lead with trust, agility, and resilience in the emerging agentic era.

### Building an open, interoperable Internet for AI agents collaboration

Tushar Agrawal, Senior Director of Product Management at Outshift by Cisco  
Tuesday, August 12 | 11:55 a.m.-12:15 p.m. PT

AI is rapidly transforming software development, and the biggest shift is the move from single model assistants to collaborative multi-agent software. This software brings together agents with different domain expertise, trained on different data sets to solve complex business and scientific problems. Imagine the problems that can be solved when AI agents can discover and trust each other, collaborate seamlessly, and be observed, regardless of how they were built or where they run—this is the promise of the Internet of Agents.

Join Tushar Agrawal, Senior Director of Product Management at Outshift by Cisco, to learn about the foundational technologies and standards that the AGNTCY collective is building for the Internet of Agents. Explore the Internet of Agents architectural components and how they work together with MCP and A2A and discuss real-world examples of multi-agent workflows.

### Tushar Agrawal, Sr. Director, Product Management, GenAI

Tushar Agrawal is Sr. Director of Product for Generative AI at Outshift by Cisco. Outshift's mission is to build what's next and new in emerging tech for Cisco and for tomorrow's customer needs. Outshift focuses creating new products and businesses for Cisco in emerging areas such as cloud native applications (security and observability), quantum, and AI. Tushar is responsible for defining and delivering on Outshift's GenAI product strategy and roadmap.

Tushar has over 14 years of product and business management experience with Fortune 10 companies and multiple early-stage venture-backed startups. Before joining Cisco, Tushar led the technical product management and go-to-market team for Amazon Web Services computer vision (CV) AI business. In this role, he drove the roadmap, development, and adoption of innovative pre-trained, AutoML, and GenAI-powered object detection, text extraction, facial analysis, content moderation, and other CV capabilities.

### Anand Raghavan, Senior Director of Engineering, AI, Security Business Group, Cisco

As Senior Director of Engineering, AI at Cisco, Anand loves the excitement of working with world-class teams to build and market game-changing products. Anand was the co-founder and Chief Product Officer of Armorblox, the industry's first GPT-powered protection against targeted email attacks. Cisco acquired Armorblox in July 2023 to bring generative AI powered assistant and automation experiences to Cisco customers. Prior to Armorblox, Anand launched ThoughtSpot out of stealth mode, and built and ran product marketing and product management teams there. Anand was a founding team member and product manager at Blue Jeans Network, helping to grow it from four employees to 200+ employees and 2,000+ customers. Before that, he held several engineering roles, including six years at NVIDIA. Anand has a B.Tech. in electrical engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, an M.S. in electrical and computer engineering from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, and an MBA from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania, where he graduated with honors and was named a Palmer Scholar.

### Chris Rowen​, VP, Engineering Collaboration AI, Cisco

Chris is a Silicon Valley entrepreneur and technologist, now VP of AI in Cisco's Collaboration business. Most recently he was co-founder and CEO of BabbleLabs, a speech ML company, acquired by Cisco in 2020. Prior to BabbleLabs, Chris served as CTO for Cadence’s IP Group, which he joined after Cadence’s acquisition of Tensilica, the company he founded in 1997 to develop extensible processors. He led Tensilica as CEO and later, CTO, to develop one of the most prolific embedded processor architectures, especially for compute-intensive embedded processing. Chris was a pioneer in developing RISC architecture and helped found MIPS Computer Systems in 1984. He has an MS and PhD in EE from Stanford and a BA in physics from Harvard. He was named an IEEE Fellow in 2015 for his work in the development of microprocessor technology.​
